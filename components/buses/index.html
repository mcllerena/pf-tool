Injections

Section titled “Injections”

Each bus has a specified complex injection in pu:

Sisp=(Pg,i−Pl,i)+j (Qg,i−Ql,i)SbaseS_i^{sp} = \frac{(P_{g,i} - P_{l,i}) + j\,(Q_{g,i} - Q_{l,i})}{S_{base}}Sisp​=Sbase​(Pg,i​−Pl,i​)+j(Qg,i​−Ql,i​)​
  • Pg, Qg, Pl, and Ql come from DBAR.
  • SVC output is added to Qg at its bus; see SVC.
  • HVDC converter powers are added to Pl/Ql at rectifiers and to Pg/Ql at inverters; see HVDC LCC.
  • Loads are constant power.
  • The bus shunt Sh (Mvar at 1 pu) becomes the susceptance bish=Sh/Sbaseb^{sh}_i = \text{Sh}/S_{base}bish​=Sh/Sbase​ on the diagonal of YbusY_{bus}Ybus​. Its injection is therefore bish ∣Vi∣2b^{sh}_i\,|V_i|^2bish​∣Vi​∣2.

Generators and reactive limits

Section titled “Generators and reactive limits”

The scheduled generation is taken from DBAR.Pg/Qg. DGER contributes only the active-power limits Pgn/Pgm, which are stored on the bus as p_min_mw/p_max_mw. Pgn is a minimum and is never added to the schedule.

Zero-width reactive range

Section titled “Zero-width reactive range”

ANAREDE writes blank reactive limits as Qgn = Qgm = 0. More than 200 Tb = 1 buses in each case have these limits. All of them have a stored output of Qg = 0; ANAREDE solved them at a zero-width limit. They are therefore built as PQ buses with fixed QgQ_gQg​.

Reactive limit enforcement (QLIM)

Section titled “Reactive limit enforcement (QLIM)”

After each converged inner solve, every PV bus checks the generation needed to hold its voltage:

Qg,ireq=Qicalc Sbase+Ql,i−QisvcQ_{g,i}^{req} = Q_i^{calc}\,S_{base} + Q_{l,i} - Q^{svc}_iQg,ireq​=Qicalc​Sbase​+Ql,i​−Qisvc​
  • If Qg,ireq>Qmax⁡+0.1Q_{g,i}^{req} > Q_{\max} + 0.1Qg,ireq​>Qmax​+0.1 Mvar, the bus becomes PQ with Qg=Qmax⁡Q_g = Q_{\max}Qg​=Qmax​.
  • If Qg,ireq<Qmin⁡−0.1Q_{g,i}^{req} < Q_{\min} - 0.1Qg,ireq​<Qmin​−0.1 Mvar, the bus becomes PQ with Qg=Qmin⁡Q_g = Q_{\min}Qg​=Qmin​.
  • The equations are then solved again. A converted bus stays PQ, so there is no back-switching.

Voltage-limited load buses (VLIM)

Section titled “Voltage-limited load buses (VLIM)”

Only buses with Tb = 3 take part. If such a bus leaves its DGLT band, Vmn–Vmx, by more than 0.001 pu, it becomes a PV bus held at the violated limit.

Earlier versions applied this rule to every PQ bus. That conflicted with the generator reactive limits and caused PV/PQ cycling, so it is now restricted to the ANAREDE Tb = 3 buses.
