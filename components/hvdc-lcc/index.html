HVDC LCC links

Source: models/hvdc_lcc.py.

The cases contain 17 LCC poles and lines. They include Itaipu (Foz do Iguaçu–Ibiúna), Madeira (Santo Antônio–Araraquara), the Porto Velho back-to-back converter, Belo Monte (Xingu–Estreito and Xingu–Terminal Rio), and the Alumar links.

The DC network is not part of the AC Newton state. Each link becomes active and reactive injections at its two AC buses. These injections are recomputed from the AC solution in the outer control passes.

Building a link

Section titled “Building a link”

For each in-service DCLI line, the solver builds the link in six steps:

  1. It finds the converter (DCNV) at each DC bus. Converter Modo = R is the rectifier and Modo = I is the inverter.
  2. It reads the controls (DCCV) of both converters. Exactly one converter must have F = "F". That converter is the DC slack: it absorbs the line losses. The other converter must use power control (TCnv = P) and holds the power order.

Updates during the solve

Section titled “Updates during the solve”

After each converged inner solve, update_lcc_from_dc_solution performs four updates:

  1. It recomputes losses and the DC voltage at the slack end.
  2. It recomputes the overlap angle μ\muμ from the present AC terminal voltages first, then computes the target QQQ at each converter from that μ\muμ. A voltage change therefore affects QQQ in the same pass.
  3. It moves each converter tap toward the value that delivers the DC voltage at the specified angle. The tap is clipped to Tmn/Tmx:
atarget=0.995⋅32π nb Vfs ∣Vac∣ cos⁡(α+μ/2)Vdc,a^{target} = \frac{0.995\cdot\frac{3\sqrt{2}}{\pi}\,n_b\,V_{fs}\,|V_{ac}|\,\cos(\alpha+\mu/2)}{V_{dc}},atarget=Vdc​0.995⋅π32​​nb​Vfs​∣Vac​∣cos(α+μ/2)​,

In this expression, nbn_bnb​ is the number of bridges (DCNV.P).

  1. It applies the changes to the bus injections. The damping factor is 1, meaning a plain fixed-point update. The link reports a change when any P or Q value moves by more than 0.1 MW/Mvar, which triggers another outer pass. If every change stays within that tolerance, the update is rolled back, so the converter powers remain exactly those used for the final AC solution and the reported state stays consistent.

For links with a base voltage of 10 kV or less, reactive power is kept at its initial value.
