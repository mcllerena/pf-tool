BIPS AC/DC Power Flow

BIPS AC/DC Power Flow

Solve AC power flows for Brazilian system ANAREDE-style .dat cases (about 12,600 buses each) with a sparse Newton-Raphson solver or an optimization model solved by Ipopt.
Getting startedOptimization formulation

What is inside

Section titled “What is inside”

Data file

AMPL-style param: BLOCK: columns := sections exported from ANAREDE .pwf cases. Every block and column used by the solver is documented.

Components

Buses, generators, lines, transformers with LTC and phase-shifter control, series compensators, shunt banks, SVCs, and HVDC LCC links.

Newton-Raphson

Sparse polar Newton method. Its state includes SVC outputs, LTC taps, and phase-shift angles. Outer passes handle discrete controls.

Optimization power flow

A Pyomo nonlinear program solved with Ipopt. It uses signed residuals with per-bus and aggregate bounds and switches SVC operating regions between solves.
