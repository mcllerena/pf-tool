Optimization power flow

Source: optimization.py (build_optimization_model, OptimizationPowerFlow).

The AC power flow is posed as a nonlinear program (NLP), following the o-grid optimization ACPF paper. Power-balance mismatches are explicit signed residual variables. Each residual is bounded by the convergence tolerance at every bus, and their sum is bounded across the system. A feasible point is therefore a converged power flow. The objective drives the residuals toward zero and lightly regularizes the state toward the initial point.

The model is built on the reduced network, solved with Ipopt, and re-solved between discrete control updates.

Objective function

Section titled “Objective function”

The objective minimizes the residuals normalized by the tolerance, plus a small regularization toward the initial state:

min⁡  ∑i∈NP(riPε)2+∑i∈NQ(riQε)2+∑s∈S(rscε)2+w∑i∈N[(vi−vi0)2+(θi−θi0)2]\min\; \sum_{i\in\mathcal N^P}\Big(\frac{r^P_i}{\varepsilon}\Big)^2 + \sum_{i\in\mathcal N^Q}\Big(\frac{r^Q_i}{\varepsilon}\Big)^2 + \sum_{s\in\mathcal S}\Big(\frac{r^c_s}{\varepsilon}\Big)^2 + w\sum_{i\in\mathcal N}\Big[(v_i - v_i^0)^2 + (\theta_i - \theta_i^0)^2\Big]mini∈NP∑​(εriP​​)2+i∈NQ∑​(εriQ​​)2+s∈S∑​(εrsc​​)2+wi∈N∑​[(vi​−vi0​)2+(θi​−θi0​)2]

Dividing by ε\varepsilonε puts residuals at the tolerance on the order of 1. With the weight w=10−3w = 10^{-3}w=10−3, the regularization only selects among near-solutions and does not trade against the balance. Scheduled active generation is fixed, so there are no redispatch terms.

Section titled “Convergence test”

After the last NLP solve, solution_metrics recomputes every residual from YbusY_{bus}Ybus​ and the solved voltages, instead of reading the residual variables. The run is reported as converged only if all of these hold:

  1. Ipopt terminated optimal, locallyOptimal, or feasible.
  2. max⁡i∣Pi−Pisp∣≤ε\max_i |P_i - P_i^{sp}| \le \varepsilonmaxi​∣Pi​−Pisp​∣≤ε over NP\mathcal N^PNP, and max⁡i∣Qi−Qisp−∑qs∣≤ε\max_i |Q_i - Q_i^{sp} - \sum q_s| \le \varepsilonmaxi​∣Qi​−Qisp​−∑qs​∣≤ε over NQ\mathcal N^QNQ.
  3. The aggregate P and Q mismatches are at most ε\varepsilonε.
  4. Every SVC equation for its region holds within ε\varepsilonε.
  5. The last control pass produced no pending changes.

Voltage magnitudes are bounded only numerically. Operating limits from DGLT are a separate acceptability check, not part of convergence.
