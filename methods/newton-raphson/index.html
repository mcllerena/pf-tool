Newton-Raphson

Source: newton_raphson.py (NewtonRaphsonPowerFlow).

Unknowns and equations

Section titled “Unknowns and equations”

At each iteration, the bus types define the index sets:

  • A\mathcal{A}A (angle buses): PV ∪ PQ;
  • Q\mathcal{Q}Q: PQ buses;
  • S\mathcal{S}S: active SVCs (device bus is PQ);
  • C\mathcal{C}C: regulating tap and phase controls (not limited, not conflicting).

The state vector and the mismatch vector are:

  • gsvcg^{svc}gsvc is the SVC control law or limit equation for its region (SVC).

Step control

Section titled “Step control”

The solver applies four safeguards to each Newton step:

  1. Conflict screening: controls whose step is non-physical are fixed, and the step is recomputed up to five times. The thresholds are ∣Δs∣>0.2|\Delta s| > 0.2∣Δs∣>0.2 for taps and >1> 1>1 rad for phases.
  2. Control step limit: if any control asks for more than 0.05 (tap) or 0.1 rad (phase), the whole step is scaled down, preserving the direction.
  3. Backtracking line search: the step scale is halved up to 16 times. A trial is rejected if any PQ magnitude leaves [0.4,2.0][0.4, 2.0][0.4,2.0] pu. It is accepted when either ∥F∥∞\lVert F\rVert_\infty∥F∥∞​ or ∥F∥2\lVert F\rVert_2∥F∥2​ decreases. As a last resort, a tiny step (≤2−15\le 2^{-15}≤2−15) is accepted. Each trial re-evaluates SVC regions and rebuilds YbusY_{bus}Ybus​ for the trial taps.
  4. Control limits: shifts are clipped to their bounds. A control that keeps pushing against a bound for 3 iterations is fixed there.

The inner solve converges when ∥F∥∞≤ε=TEPA/BASE\lVert F\rVert_\infty \le \varepsilon = \text{TEPA}/\text{BASE}∥F∥∞​≤ε=TEPA/BASE. It fails when it reaches the iteration limit ACIT, the matrix is singular, or a voltage leaves [0.4,2.0][0.4, 2.0][0.4,2.0] pu.

Outer control passes

Section titled “Outer control passes”

The result is converged only when the final inner solve meets the tolerance and no control changed in the final pass. Typical runs take 11–19 Newton iterations in total over all passes, in about 2.5–3 s, including parsing.
